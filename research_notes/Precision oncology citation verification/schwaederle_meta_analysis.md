### Takeaway
The 31% vs 10.5% response-rate figure, the 5.9 vs 2.7-month PFS figure, and a 13.7 vs 8.9-month OS figure all belong to **one single paper**: Schwaederle M, Zhao M, Lee JJ, Eggermont AM, Schilsky RL, Mendelsohn J, Lazar V, Kurzrock R. **"Impact of Precision Medicine in Diverse Cancers: A Meta-Analysis of Phase II Clinical Trials."** *Journal of Clinical Oncology*, 2015, Vol. 33, Issue 32, pp. 3817–3825. This is a **different, separate paper** from "Association of Biomarker-Based Treatment Strategies With Response Rates and Progression-Free Survival in Refractory Malignant Neoplasms: A Meta-analysis," which is a 2016 *JAMA Oncology* paper by the same lead author covering **phase I** trials with **different numbers**. ## Caveat on source access

Direct, first-hand retrieval of the rendered article page from PMC (PMC4737863), ascopubs.org (the JCO publisher's own site), and PubMed (26304871) was attempted multiple times in this research session; all three returned anti-bot verification screens or cookie-consent walls instead of article content, and could not be bypassed by the automated fetch tool available. The figures and verbatim-adjacent language reported above were instead obtained through the Europe PMC REST/JSON API (`www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/...`), which mirrors the same abstract record and returned the PMID, DOI, PMCID, and abstract content programmatically, cross-checked against independent secondary aggregators (scinapse.io, institutional repository listings). This is a legitimate structured-data source, not a blog or press summary, but it is not a human-eyes-on-the-PMC-page confirmation. For a presentation slide requiring an exact verbatim quote and a clickable, citable link, recommend linking to the DOI (https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2015.61.5997) or PMC4737863, and recommend the presenter do a quick manual browser check (completing the CAPTCHA/cookie prompt) before the talk to visually confirm the abstract text matches, since this session could not get past that wall itself.
